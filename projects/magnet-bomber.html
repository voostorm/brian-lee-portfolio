Games & graphics / Sep 2025

Magnet Bomber

An arena prototype built around pulling and launching bombs to set up explosions.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Raycasts
  • WebGL

3-person team · 2 weeks · USC

01 / The problem

What this project explores

Our team prototyped a combat loop where magnetic interactions change how the player positions bombs and handles threats.

02 / My contribution

The work I took on

Worked on a Unity arena prototype with player controls, bomb behavior, targeting feedback, and collision handling.

  • The player controller combines mouse-directed movement, rotation, boundary clamping, and aim/readiness indicators.
  • Bomb behavior includes launch speed, acceleration, bounce, lifetime, and explosion radius.
  • Layer-masked raycasts and line-rendered indicators support targeting and interaction feedback.

What it demonstrates
